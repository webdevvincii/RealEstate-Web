About Prestige Estates

Redefining Luxury
Real Estate

Since 2005, Prestige Estates has been the benchmark for luxury real estate excellence. We don't just sell homes — we curate lifestyles. Our team of handpicked specialists brings unmatched market insight, discretion, and a genuine passion for connecting people with extraordinary spaces.

From Manhattan penthouses and Malibu cliffside retreats to sun-drenched Miami estates, we handle every detail with white-glove precision — so you can focus on imagining your future, not navigating the process.
